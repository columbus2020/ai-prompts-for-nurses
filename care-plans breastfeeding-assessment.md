# Breastfeeding Assessment Guide

**Mother Questions:
- How many wet diapers per day?
- Any nipple pain or cracks?
- Feeding frequency?

**Baby Signs of Good Feeding:**
1. 6+ wet diapers
2. Swallowing sound heard
3. Content after feeding

**AI Prompt to Use:**
"Act as a lactation consultant. Assess this breastfeeding case: [DESCRIBE CASE] and give me 3 interventions."

**Red Flags to Refer:**
- No weight gain
- Dehydration signs
- Severe pain
